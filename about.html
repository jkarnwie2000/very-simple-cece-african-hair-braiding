Who We Are

Braiding is more than hair. It's who we are.

Cece African Hair Braiding was born from a lifelong love of the craft — the kind of love that begins as a child sitting between a mother's knees, learning patience one braid at a time.

That tradition lives on in our salon. We combine time-honoured African techniques with today's most-loved styles to create looks that are beautiful, protective and uniquely yours. Whether it's your first set of braids or your fiftieth, you'll be welcomed like family.

— Cece & the team

Our Heritage

Inspired by generations of African artistry

Across Africa, braiding has been practised for thousands of years. Styles have long expressed identity, community, age and celebration, and braiding sessions bring families and friends together.

From the elegant Fulani braids of West Africa to bold, sculpted Ghana braids and classic cornrows, the styles we create carry that history forward. We honour it by doing the work with care, respect and attention to detail.

“Every braid carries a story — of where we come from, and who we choose to be.”
