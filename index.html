Welcome to Cece

Where African tradition meets modern style

Cece African Hair Braiding is a warm, welcoming salon devoted to the art of braiding. Every style is crafted by hand with clean parts, even tension and finishing touches that make your look last.

  • Protective styles that respect the health of your natural hair
  • Braids, twists, cornrows, locs and kids styles under one roof
  • Honest consultations, so you know what to expect before we begin
