Before You Arrive

Preparing for your appointment

Come with clean hair

Please arrive with hair washed, conditioned, fully dried and detangled, unless you've booked a wash.

Bring inspiration

Photos of the length, size and colour you love help us get your style exactly right.

Plan for the time

Larger styles can take several hours. Bring a charger, headphones or a good book.

Running late?

Give us a call at (555) 123-4567 so we can plan around you.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is braiding hair included in the price?

It depends on the style. When you request an appointment, let us know if you'd like us to provide the hair and we'll include it in your quote.

How long will my braids last?

Most braided styles last 4–8 weeks with good care. We'll share aftercare tips so your style stays neat for as long as possible.

Do you braid children's hair?

Yes! We love working with kids ages 3 and up. We keep sessions gentle and break longer styles into a comfortable pace.

Can I request a custom style?

Absolutely. Choose "Not sure / consultation" on the appointment form and describe your idea in the comments.

Do I pay online?

No. This website is for appointment requests only — we never ask for payment details online. Payment is handled at the salon.

Found a style you love?

Send us an appointment request and we'll be in touch to confirm your date, time and price.
